# English Clapback Arsenal

This file catalogs high-engagement English comebacks, organized by style. All entries are curated from publicly circulated internet culture and selected for wit, brevity, and devastating effect.

When using, match the opponent's energy — a troll needs a savage burn, a condescending colleague needs a chill deflection. Pick 2-3 fitting options and adapt to context.

---

## Savage Burns

Maximum damage, minimum words. Use when someone deserves a direct hit.

- Your opinion's cute — too bad it's irrelevant.
- I'd agree with you, but then we'd both be wrong.
- Mirrors can't talk. Lucky for you, they can't laugh.
- You bring everyone so much joy — when you leave.
- Jealousy looks terrible on you.
- I'm not insulting you — I'm describing you.
- Your secrets are safe — I wasn't listening.
- Keep rolling your eyes — you might find a brain.
- You're the reason God created the middle finger.
- I'd explain, but I'm out of crayons.
- You're proof evolution can go backward.
- I envy people who've never met you.
- Your birth certificate's an apology letter.
- You're not stupid — just allergic to thinking.

## Sassy Snaps

Dismissive, unbothered, devastating. Use when someone isn't worth a real argument.

- Oh, you're talking? I thought that was noise.
- Cute story — next caller.
- I'm busy — can I ignore you later?
- Your vibe's off — try a refund.
- Noted. Filed under "don't care."
- Bold of you to assume I was listening.
- I'd clap, but my hands are full of better things.
- You're speaking — still not caring.
- Try again when you make sense.
- That's adorable — now run along.

## Clever Claps

Intelligence as a weapon. Use against someone who thinks they're smarter than they are.

- If ignorance is bliss, you're in paradise.
- You must be exhausted — from being wrong all day.
- I see the "vacancy" sign's still up.
- Your IQ called — it's lost.
- I'd roast you, but my mom said be kind to idiots.
- You're the loading screen of life.
- You're the human version of a 404 error.
- Common sense called — it's not coming back.
- You put the "duh" in dumb.
- Thoughts? Yeah, didn't think so.

## Chill Deflections

Zero energy, maximum unbothered. Use when engaging would only legitimize the attack.

- Cool story — still not my problem.
- Thanks for the input — trash taken out.
- I'll consider that — right after never.
- Appreciate the effort — still irrelevant.
- Noted — moving on.
- You tried — gold star for participation.
- Interesting — still not caring.
- That's one opinion — mine's different.
- Good talk — let's not do it again.
- Heard you — still unbothered.

## Ego Checks

For the arrogant, the self-important, the main-character-syndrome cases.

- Your ego's writing checks your skills can't cash.
- Confidence is quiet — you wouldn't know.
- You're not the main character — side quest at best.
- Your crown's plastic — dollar store royalty.
- Humble's a foreign language to you.
- You're overrated — like a bad sequel.
- Your hype train derailed — years ago.
- You're the king of nothing — congrats.
- Your pedestal's made of delusions.
- Reality called — you're still on hold.

## Self-Love Slams

For when someone tries to dim your shine. Unbothered supremacy.

- I'm too busy loving me to hate you.
- My standards are high — you're not even close.
- I'm the upgrade — you're the demo.
- I shine — you reflect.
- I'm the main character — you're the extra.
- My crown's heavy — yours is plastic.
- I'm the vibe — you're the echo.
- I level up — you level down.
- I'm the prize — you're the participation trophy.
- I'm the trend — you're the try-hard.

## IQ Droppers

For intellectual condescension. Subtle, cutting, undeniable.

- Your brain's on vacation — permanently.
- Thinking isn't your strong suit — obviously.
- Intelligence skipped your generation.
- You're the reason instructions have pictures.
- Your logic's so broken, it needs tech support.
- You're living proof brains aren't mandatory.
- Your thoughts are on mute.
- Brain not found — try rebooting.

## Classic Q&A Templates

When someone throws a specific line, here's the instant counter.

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| You're so sensitive. | My sensitivity is a gift. Your obliviousness is a blessing. We're even. |
| Nobody likes you. | You have fans? Your mirror's too scared to tell you the truth. |
| You think you're so smart. | I don't think it — I just stopped pretending for your benefit. |
| You're being dramatic. | I'm being clear. You're just not used to people not tolerating you. |
| I'm just being honest. | Honesty without kindness is just cruelty with a PR team. |
| You'll understand when you're older. | I understood the day I stopped taking advice from people like you. |
| That's just your opinion. | And yet it's still more valuable than your input. |
| You're overreacting. | You're under-delivering. Let's call it even. |

---

## Usage Notes

- **Savage Burns** and **IQ Droppers** are high-damage — reserve for genuine disrespect, not playful banter.
- **Chill Deflections** are the safest default — they shut down without escalating.
- **Self-Love Slams** work best in social media contexts where confidence reads as power.
- Always pair the line with calm delivery. The same words hit harder when spoken without anger.
